Trustworthy AI for Cybersecurity: Explainability, Robustness, and Privacy

A structured review of how trustworthy-AI properties are studied in cybersecurity — covering explainability, adversarial robustness, and privacy-preserving learning across intrusion detection, malware analysis, anomaly detection, and related tasks.

  • Screening and synthesis framed around PRISMA-style review methodology across recent AI/ML cybersecurity literature.
  • Multidimensional taxonomy and conceptual framing for explainability, robustness, privacy, integrated, and deployment-oriented approaches.
  • Focus on evaluation gaps: accuracy vs interpretability, robustness, privacy, analyst usability, and computational cost.
Trustworthy AI XAI Adversarial Robustness Privacy-Preserving ML Cybersecurity
In progress Empirical study

Lightweight Federated Intrusion Detection with Post-hoc Explainability for Resource-Constrained IoT Networks

Evaluating a compact federated IDS for IoT settings where devices cannot afford heavy models — measuring detection quality, resource use, communication cost, and post-hoc feature attributions for analyst-facing clarity.

  • Federated Averaging across non-IID clients on a multi-class IoT intrusion benchmark, with mutual-information feature selection.
  • Strong aggregate metrics in the studied setup (~91.4% accuracy) while remaining lightweight in latency and memory; pruning retains most performance.
  • Post-hoc SHAP attributions for feature-level explanation, with open challenges around minority-class detection and explanation fidelity.

FAQ

Questions clients ask first

Straight answers on timelines, payment, revisions, and what I don’t take on.

How long does a typical project take?

Landing pages and small business sites usually take 1–3 weeks. Full-stack products and e-commerce are more like 4–10 weeks, depending on features. VR / AI work is scoped after a discovery call.

How do payments work?

For most packages: 40% to start, 40% at the main review milestone, 20% on launch. Hourly work can be weekly or milestone-based. Bank transfer and common local methods are fine.

How many revision rounds are included?

Packages include planned review rounds (usually 2). Extra redesigns or scope changes are quoted separately so the schedule stays honest.

Do you work with clients outside Pakistan?

Yes. Pricing is shown in PKR with USD / EUR / GBP / AED / SAR previews. Communication stays on WhatsApp, email, or video call — whichever you prefer.

What do you not take on?

I skip vague “make me an app like X” briefs with no budget or timeline, and I don’t copy competitors’ brands or content. Clear goals and respectful collaboration work best.
